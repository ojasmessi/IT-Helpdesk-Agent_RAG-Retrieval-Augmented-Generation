# Password Reset and Account Lockout

## Resetting a forgotten password
1. Go to the self-service portal at https://passwordreset.contoso.example
2. Enter your work email address and click **Next**.
3. Approve the MFA prompt on your phone (Microsoft Authenticator).
4. Choose a new password. It must be at least 14 characters and include a number and a symbol.
5. Wait 5 minutes, then sign in again on all devices (laptop, phone email, VPN).

## Account locked out
Your account locks for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts. Wait 15 minutes and try again.
If you are still locked out, the old password is probably saved on another device (often a phone mail app).
Remove the account from your phone, wait 15 minutes, then sign in again.

## Password expiry
Passwords expire every 90 days. You get a reminder email 14 days before expiry.
You cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords.
