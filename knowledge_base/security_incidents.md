# Reporting Security Incidents

## Suspicious email (phishing)
Do not click links or open attachments. Use the **Report Phishing** button in Outlook. This sends it to the Security team.

## I clicked a phishing link or entered my password
1. Disconnect from the network (turn off Wi-Fi) if you downloaded or opened a file.
2. Reset your password immediately from a different, trusted device.
3. Contact the Service Desk by phone on extension 5000 — this is treated as a Priority 1 incident.

## Lost or stolen laptop or phone
Report it within 1 hour to the Service Desk (extension 5000). The device will be remotely wiped. Also report theft to local police.

## Malware or strange pop-ups
Stop using the device, disconnect it from the network and call extension 5000. Do not try to clean it yourself.
