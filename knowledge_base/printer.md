# Printing

## Adding an office printer
All office printers use Follow-Me printing. Install the "Contoso-FollowMe" printer from Company Portal (Windows) or Self Service (Mac).
Send the job, then tap your badge on any office printer to release it. Jobs are held for 24 hours.

## Print job stuck or nothing prints
1. Check you tapped your badge on the printer.
2. Remove and re-add the Contoso-FollowMe printer.
3. On Windows, restart the "Print Spooler" service or restart the laptop.

## Printer out of toner or paper jam
Report it to Facilities using the QR code on the printer — not to IT.
