# Email and Outlook Issues

## Outlook keeps asking for my password
1. Close Outlook.
2. Open Windows **Credential Manager** > Windows Credentials and remove entries starting with "MicrosoftOffice".
3. Restart Outlook and sign in once with MFA.

## Mailbox full
The mailbox limit is 50 GB. Empty **Deleted Items** and turn on the Online Archive (File > Tools > Mailbox Cleanup).

## Setting up email on a phone
Install the Microsoft Outlook app from the App Store or Google Play. The built-in phone mail apps are blocked by policy.
You must enrol the phone in Intune Company Portal first.

## Shared mailbox access
Shared mailbox access must be approved by the mailbox owner. Raise a ticket naming the shared mailbox and the owner.
